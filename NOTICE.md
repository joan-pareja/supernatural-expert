# Third-party notices

> Keep third-party attribution here. Do not mix it with the code license.

The corpus contains adapted text from English Wikipedia. Wikipedia text is
generally available under the [Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0
International license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/) and may
also have other terms shown on its source pages.

Each stored episode document must keep its article title, canonical URL, page
ID, revision ID, retrieval time, and license marker. Answers must link to the
source articles. Cleaned or combined text must be marked as modified.

The MIT license covers the application code, not Wikipedia text. Any cleaned
corpus export included with the project keeps the CC BY-SA 4.0 terms.

See Wikipedia's [reuse guidance](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Wikipedia:Copyrights#Reusers'_rights_and_obligations)
and the Wikimedia Foundation [Terms of Use](https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Terms_of_Use).

Images and other fair-use media are not part of the corpus.
